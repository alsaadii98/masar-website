Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can search, navigate and post reports as a guest. Log in only to keep points and sync your saved places.

How do road reports work?

Tap Report and pick a type. Drivers nearby see it on the map with its place and time. Your name is never shown.

Does Masar work with CarPlay and Android Auto?

Yes. Connect your phone to your car and open Masar on the car screen.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → My account → Delete account. Or see Delete account.
